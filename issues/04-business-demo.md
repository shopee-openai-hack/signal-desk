- Validate against INT-S1–S13 and update confirmed decisions in `INTENT.md`.

## Open-question ownership

- Own INT-Q1, Q2, Q4, Q5, and Q9.
- Review source representativeness for A's Q3.

## Acceptance criteria

- The scenario shows pending evidence, material update, approval, and actual delisting.
- It also contains a downgrade/refutation path and does not force escalation.
- Real external evidence, simulated platform data, and controlled replay are labeled.
- Expected outputs serve as evaluation examples, not hard-coded model answers.
- The demo shows work saved for an employee without claiming real platform actions,
  general cross-department orchestration, proactive alerts, or cross-case learning.

First handoff to A/B/C: selected scenario, source/evidence inputs, product records,
priority examples, and the exact demo stages.
